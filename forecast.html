Project · semiconductor-forecast

In Progress

Semiconductor Revenue. Forecasting

Extracting clean quarterly revenue from SEC EDGAR XBRL filings as the foundation for out-of-sample revenue forecasting — a free, reproducible alternative to paid data vendors.

01 · Overview

What problem it solves

Forecasting the next quarter's revenue for US-listed semiconductor companies usually means either buying a data vendor's feed or scraping unstructured sources. This project instead builds the dataset directly from the SEC's official, machine-readable EDGAR XBRL filings — free, unaudited-by-a-middleman, and updated every time a company files.

The immediate goal is a clean quarterly revenue series per issuer, because that series is the prerequisite for any forecasting model. The forecasting and backtest layers are scoped but not yet implemented (see Methodology).

Status: data-acquisition and quarterly-extraction layers are implemented and verified on a single issuer; multi-company collection, the forecasting model and the walk-forward backtest are in progress.

02 · Data Sources

Where the data comes from

SEC EDGAR companyfacts

Per-company XBRL-tagged facts (revenue, and every other US-GAAP line item), served as JSON. Unauthenticated and free. The revenue tag itself drifts between filings, so the extractor merges across tags rather than trusting a single one.

SEC company_tickers.json

The ticker → CIK directory, used to map a symbol (e.g. NVDA) to its CIK (1045810) before fetching companyfacts.

Coverage

A configured universe of ~18 US-listed semiconductor issuers (NVDA, AMD, AVGO, QCOM, MU, WDC, STX, TXN, ADI, NXPI, MCHP, ON, INTC, AMAT, LRCX, KLAC and ASML). Foreign private issuers that file only annual 20-F reports (TSMC, ASML) are excluded from the quarterly series because they have no quarterly revenue facts.

Update model & limits

  • Fetched on demand; every response is archived to a content-addressed gzip under data/raw/edgar before parsing, so any figure can be traced to the exact bytes.
  • Q4 is never filed on its own — a 10-Q covers Q1–Q3 only, so Q4 must be derived from the annual 10-K.
  • The archive is a point-in-time snapshot; it is not a live feed.

03 · Methodology

How it works

SEC EDGAR companyfacts (XBRL JSON)
        │  fetch + archive (URL-hash cache)
        ▼
content-addressed gzip  →  data/raw/edgar/*.json.gz
        │  merge revenue tags · classify by duration
        ▼
quarterly revenue  +  derived fiscal Q4
        │  upsert (idempotent)
        ▼
SQLite  revenue_quarterly · prices_daily · collection_runs
        │
        ▼  (planned, not yet built)
Yahoo benchmarks  ──▶  baseline / model  ──▶  walk-forward backtest

Implemented

  • Fetch + archive. companyfacts JSON is downloaded and written to disk before parsing, so provenance is preserved.
  • Revenue-tag priority chain, merged. A priority list of six revenue tags is tried, but the extractor merges facts across tags rather than picking the first non-empty one — so a filer that switches tags mid-history (NVIDIA moved off its old tag around 2020) does not silently truncate the series.
  • Duration classification. Facts of ~3 months (80–100 days) are quarterly; ~12 months (330–400 days) are annual and used only to derive Q4.
  • Q4 derivation. Q4 = annual (10-K) − sum of the three prior filed quarters, matched by ordering rather than calendar subtraction to tolerate non-calendar fiscal years.
  • Deduplication. When a period is filed more than once, the most recently filed version wins.

Planned (config exists, code not yet written)

  • Collect sector benchmark prices (SMH / SOXX via Yahoo) as candidate predictors.
  • Baseline model: previous-year same-quarter revenue as a naive seasonal point forecast. This is deliberately not a year-over-year growth-rate extrapolation — the two are distinct and will be benchmarked separately.
  • Walk-forward backtest with a horizon of one quarter (HORIZON_QUARTERS=1), a minimum history of 12 quarters, and a 20-quarter minimum training window.

04 · Results

What's verifiable today

No forecasting or backtest results exist yet. The forecasting model and walk-forward evaluation are not implemented, so there are no accuracy metrics to report — and none are fabricated here.

Data-layer verification (NVIDIA, CIK 1045810)

The extractor was run against the archived NVIDIA companyfacts snapshot. 05 · Limitations & Next Steps

Known limits and what's next

Limitations

  • Only a single-issuer (NVIDIA) snapshot has been archived; the full universe has not been collected.
  • The derived-Q4 figure has not been reconciled against audited figures.
  • No forecasting model or backtest exists yet, so nothing here has been validated out-of-sample.
  • Tag classification uses fixed duration windows; unusual fiscal periods could fall outside them.

Next steps

  • Run collection across the full universe into SQLite.
  • Wire the Yahoo benchmark-price collector as a predictor source.
  • Implement the naive seasonal baseline and a walk-forward backtest, then benchmark it separately from a YoY-growth extrapolation baseline to avoid conflating the two.
  • Validate derived-Q4 against 10-K totals.
